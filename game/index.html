EMBERLINE

A round-based tactical shooter in a derelict desert foundry. You lead the Jackals: carry the charge to the Kiln Yard or the Vault, plant it, and hold the line — or wipe the Wardens out. Win rounds, earn money, buy better guns. First team to 7 rounds takes the foundry.

CONTROLS

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look · Click Fire
  • Shift Walk (quiet, steady)
  • Ctrl / C Crouch
  • Space Jump
  • R Reload · B Buy menu
  • 1 2 Primary / sidearm
  • 3 4 5 Frag / flash / smoke
  • E hold Plant the charge
  • RMB Aim (Osprey scope)

THE BRIEF

Stopping to shoot is how you hit anything: spread blooms while you move, jump or spray. Headshots count four times. Your squad hears gunfire and rotates — so does theirs. The charge takes 3.5 seconds to plant and 35 to blow; a Warden needs 5 quiet seconds to defuse it.
